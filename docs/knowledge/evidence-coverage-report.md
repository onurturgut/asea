# Volume 01 Evidence Coverage Report

## Purpose

This report demonstrates complete Evidence relationship coverage across every
Volume 01 Concept, Chapter, Module, Learning Outcome, Assessment, Active Source,
and authority tier.

## Scope

The analysis joins fourteen operational Evidence records to the frozen Volume
01 curriculum as of 2026-07-18. It measures candidate support relationships,
not approved Claims.
