# Volume 01 Concept Categories

## Purpose

This document defines the controlled category view used to organize Volume 01
Concept candidates without creating a new category standard.

## Scope

Categories classify Concepts for discovery and review. They do not determine
prerequisite direction or replace Module and Chapter ownership.

## Ownership

- **Authority:** Derived classification view.
- **Source:** [Concept Candidate Registry](./concept-registry.md).
- **Owner:** Knowledge Architect.
- **Category changes:** Require Concept Registry review.
