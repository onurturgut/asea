# Volume 01 Knowledge Learning Paths

## Purpose

This document turns the validated Concept topology into a deterministic learner
progression without changing the frozen curriculum order.

## Scope

The path begins with prerequisite roots, follows the frozen Chapter sequence,
uses direct and indirect dependencies as readiness checks, and integrates each
Concept through its Lab, Assessment, and Project mappings.

## Ownership

- **Authority:** Derived study path.
- **Graph source:** [Knowledge Graph Candidate Registry](./knowledge-graph-registry.md).
- **Curriculum source:** `V01-BP01`, version `1.1.0`.
- **Generated:** 2026-07-18.
- **Owner:** Knowledge Architect.

## Content

### Plan Selection

The canonical path is:

1. Complete every direct and indirect prerequisite.
2. Study Concepts in frozen Chapter order.
3. Apply the Chapter Concepts in the mapped Lab.
4. Verify them through the mapped Assessment.
5. Integrate them through the mapped Mini Project or Capstone.

Entry begins with `ASEA-CON-000001`, `ASEA-CON-000007`. Concepts must not be skipped when they
appear in the prerequisite closure of a later target.

### Adjustment Rules

- A learner may repeat a Chapter, Lab, or Assessment without changing graph order.
- A target Concept requires its full direct and indirect prerequisite closure.
- An assessment failure returns the learner to the failed Concept and its direct prerequisites.
- A project may integrate earlier Concepts but cannot waive prerequisite or assessment gates.
- Candidate edges guide planning but require operational approval before automated high-stakes decisions.
