# Programming Fundamentals Academy Educational Review

## Review Scope

Review progression, beginner suitability, difficulty, concept order, workload,
theory/practice balance, Project placement, and assessment placement.

## Inputs

- [Academy Overview](../academy/01-academy-overview.md)
- [Learning Path](../academy/02-learning-path.md)
- [Lesson Registry](../academy/05-lesson-registry.md)
- [Statistics](../academy/13-academy-statistics.md)
- [Validation Report](./00-curriculum-validation-report.md)

## Validation Method

Compare the learning sequence with prerequisite depth, Bloom progression,
practice ratios, and evidence gates. Review all 38 Lesson records and 12 Module
transitions.

## Findings

### EDU-001

- Severity: Observation
- Status: Open, non-blocking
- Evidence: Total learner workload is 209-236 hours although the guided core is
  110 hours.
- Impact: Learners may misread the 100-120-hour core as the full Academy load.
- Owner: Curriculum Architect
- Required resolution: Preserve the separate core/Project workload display in
  future learner-facing planning.

## Decision

**Approved.** Progression is suitable for beginners, complexity increases
gradually, and practice grows from guided tracing to independent delivery.
This decision does not override the global validation failures.

## Required Actions

Carry `EDU-001` into the next Study Plan presentation.
