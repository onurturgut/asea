# ASEA Learning Outcome Registry

## Purpose

This registry indexes every canonical Learning Outcome without duplicating or
rewording its authoritative measurable statement.

## Scope

The registry covers `V01-LO001` through `V01-LO046`, their Module, Bloom level,
and primary evidence. Proposed migration outcomes are excluded because they are
not canonical.

## Ownership

- **Authority:** Derived.
- **Authoritative outcome statements:** `V01-SD-LO-001`.
- **Meaning changes:** Forbidden without approved curriculum migration.
- **Registry rule:** IDs and evidence labels are indexed; canonical statements
  remain in their authoritative source.
